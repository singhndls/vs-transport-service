# Chota Hathi vs Bolero Pickup: Which One Do You Need?

One of the most common questions we get at VS Transport is: *"Should I book a Chota Hathi or a Bolero Pickup?"*

It's a fair question. Both are popular light commercial vehicles used for goods transport in Delhi NCR. But they serve different purposes — and booking the wrong one can mean either overpaying or struggling to fit your load.

Here's a clear breakdown to help you decide.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Chota Hathi | Bolero Pickup |
|---|---|---|
| Load capacity | Up to 750 kg | Up to 1,500 kg |
| Body length | ~8 feet | ~7–8 feet |
| Best for | Light goods, narrow lanes | Medium loads, regular routes |
| Fuel type | Diesel | Diesel |
| Ideal distance | Up to 50 km | Up to 150 km |
| Cost (approx.) | ₹600–₹1,200 | ₹900–₹1,800 |

*Prices vary based on distance, load, and time of booking.*

## What is a Chota Hathi?

The Chota Hathi (literally "small elephant") is a compact mini-truck — typically a Tata Ace or similar vehicle. It's the go-to choice for:

- **Small household shifts** — 1 BHK or studio apartment moves
- **Intra-city deliveries** — within Delhi or within Noida
- **Narrow lane access** — markets, old Delhi lanes, congested industrial areas
- **Light commercial goods** — garments, packed boxes, small appliances
- **E-commerce last-mile** — delivering bulk orders to a single location

**When to choose Chota Hathi:**
If your goods weigh under 700 kg and you're moving within the city, Chota Hathi is the most cost-effective choice.

## What is a Bolero Pickup?

The Mahindra Bolero Pickup is a slightly larger and more powerful vehicle. It handles heavier loads and longer distances with ease. It's ideal for:

- **Medium household shifts** — 2 BHK moves with furniture
- **Factory-to-warehouse runs** — regular commercial cargo
- **Construction materials** — tiles, pipes, hardware items
- **Inter-city deliveries** — Delhi to Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad
- **Bulk goods** — larger cartons, machinery parts, raw materials

**When to choose Bolero Pickup:**
If your load is between 750 kg and 1,500 kg, or if you need to travel beyond 50 km, the Bolero Pickup is the better choice.

## 5 Questions to Help You Decide

**1. How much do your goods weigh?**
Under 700 kg → Chota Hathi. Over 700 kg → Bolero Pickup.

**2. How far are you going?**
Within the city → either works. Inter-city → Bolero Pickup.

**3. Are there access restrictions at pickup or drop?**
Narrow lanes or low-clearance areas → Chota Hathi.

**4. What type of goods are you moving?**
Light packed items → Chota Hathi. Heavy or bulky items → Bolero Pickup.

**5. What's your budget?**
Chota Hathi is typically 20–30% cheaper for the same distance.
