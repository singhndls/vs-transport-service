# How to Choose the Right Warehouse in Delhi NCR for Your Business

For businesses operating in Delhi NCR — whether you're in retail, manufacturing, e-commerce, or distribution — warehousing is a critical part of your supply chain. The right warehouse saves money, reduces delivery times, and keeps your inventory safe. The wrong one does the opposite.

Here's a practical guide to help you make the right choice.

## Why Warehousing Location Matters More Than You Think

In Delhi NCR, location directly impacts your delivery speed and transport costs. Consider:

- **Okhla Industrial Area:** Excellent for businesses serving South Delhi, Faridabad, and Noida
- **Gurgaon (NH-48 corridor):** Ideal for companies with clients in Gurgaon, Manesar, and Rewari
- **Noida / Greater Noida:** Best for e-commerce businesses serving East Delhi and UP
- **Rohini / Narela:** Cost-effective for North Delhi and Haryana routes

A warehouse that's 10 km closer to your primary delivery zone can reduce per-trip transport costs by 15–25%.

## Key Factors to Evaluate

### 1. Storage Capacity and Flexibility

Don't just look at current needs — think 12–18 months ahead. Ask:

- What is the total floor area and usable storage volume?
- Can you scale up or down based on seasonal demand?
- Are there separate zones for different product types?

Businesses with seasonal spikes (festive stock, agricultural goods) need flexible contracts, not rigid annual commitments.

### 2. Security Infrastructure

Your goods are only as safe as the facility protecting them. A good warehouse should have:

- **24/7 CCTV surveillance** covering all entry/exit points and storage areas
- **Security guards** on-site round the clock
- **Access control** — only authorised personnel can enter
- **Fire suppression systems** — sprinklers or CO2 systems
- **Proper lighting** in all areas, including loading docks

Always ask for a physical tour before signing any agreement.

### 3. Inventory Management Systems

Modern warehouses use digital inventory tracking. This means:

- Real-time stock visibility via software or app
- Barcode/QR scanning for inbound and outbound goods
- Accurate stock counts that reduce shrinkage
- Easy reconciliation for GST and accounting purposes

If a warehouse still runs on manual registers, that's a red flag for businesses handling significant volumes.

### 4. Loading and Unloading Infrastructure

Efficient loading docks save hours every week. Look for:

- Dock levellers for seamless truck loading
- Forklift availability for heavy pallets
- Wide internal lanes for vehicle movement
- Covered loading areas to protect goods from rain

Poor loading infrastructure leads to delays, damaged goods, and frustrated drivers.

### 5. Connectivity and Transport Access

Your warehouse should be easy to reach for your transport fleet:

- Located near major highways (NH-44, NH-48, NH-58, Yamuna Expressway)
- Accessible for large vehicles (avoid narrow lanes)
- Close to your primary suppliers or customers
- Good connectivity to metro or rail for staff

## Questions to Ask Before Signing a Contract

Before committing to any warehouse, ask these questions:

1. **What is the minimum contract period?** (Avoid being locked in for 2+ years without a trial)
2. **How is storage billed?** (Per pallet, per sq ft, or per cubic metre?)
3. **What are the in/out handling charges?**
4. **Is GST invoicing available?** (Critical for input tax credit)
5. **What is the claims process for damaged or missing goods?**
6. **Are there any hidden charges?** (Overtime, weekend access, etc.)

## The Cost of Getting It Wrong

Businesses that choose warehouses based on price alone often face:

- Damaged goods from poor storage conditions
- Inventory discrepancies that take weeks to resolve
- Delayed dispatches due to poor loading infrastructure
- No accountability when goods go missing

A slightly higher-cost, well-managed facility almost always works out cheaper in the long run.
