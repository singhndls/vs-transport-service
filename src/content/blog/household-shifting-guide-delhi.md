# Complete Guide to Household Shifting in Delhi NCR (2026)

Moving home is one of the most stressful experiences in life — and in Delhi NCR, with its traffic, narrow lanes, and apartment complexes, it comes with its own unique challenges. But with the right planning and the right transport partner, your shift can be smooth, affordable, and even stress-free.

This guide covers everything you need to know about household shifting in Delhi NCR in 2026.

## Step 1: Plan Your Move at Least 1 Week in Advance

Last-minute moves are expensive and chaotic. Start planning at least 7–10 days before your move date:

- **Confirm your new address** and check access for vehicles (gate width, parking, lift availability)
- **Inventory your belongings** — list everything that needs to move
- **Decide what to sell or donate** — moving is a great time to declutter
- **Book your transport** — good vehicles get booked fast, especially on weekends and month-ends

## Step 2: Choose the Right Vehicle

The vehicle you need depends on the size of your home:

| Home Size | Recommended Vehicle | Approx. Capacity |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 1 BHK | Chota Hathi | Up to 1 tonne |
| 2 BHK | Bolero Pickup or Chota Hathi (2 trips) | 1–2 tonnes |
| 3 BHK | Bolero Pickup or small truck | 2–3 tonnes |
| 4 BHK+ | Large truck or fleet vehicle | 3+ tonnes |

For most 1–2 BHK shifts in Delhi NCR, a **Chota Hathi** is the most cost-effective option. It's compact enough to navigate narrow lanes in areas like Lajpat Nagar, Karol Bagh, and Saket, yet spacious enough for a full 1 BHK.

## Step 3: Pack Smart — Room by Room

Good packing is the difference between goods arriving safely and arriving broken. Here's a room-by-room approach:

### Kitchen
- Wrap all crockery individually in newspaper or bubble wrap
- Pack glasses upright, never on their sides
- Use original boxes for appliances where possible
- Keep knives in a separate, clearly labelled box

### Bedroom
- Disassemble bed frames and wardrobes where possible
- Use wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes
- Wrap mirrors and glass furniture in thick cloth or bubble wrap
- Keep mattresses in plastic covers to protect from dust

### Living Room
- Wrap TV screens in the original box or thick foam
- Disassemble sofa legs if possible
- Label all cables and electronics before packing

### General Tips
- Use colour-coded labels per room (red = kitchen, blue = bedroom, etc.)
- Number your boxes and keep a master list
- Pack an "essentials box" — items you'll need immediately on arrival (kettle, charger, toiletries)

## Step 4: Understand the Costs

Household shifting costs in Delhi NCR vary based on:

- **Distance:** Within the same area vs. cross-city (e.g., Dwarka to Noida)
- **Volume of goods:** Number of rooms and items
- **Vehicle type:** Chota Hathi vs. Bolero vs. large truck
- **Floor and lift access:** Ground floor is cheaper; high floors with no lift cost more
- **Time of day/week:** Weekday shifts are typically 10–15% cheaper than weekends

**Typical cost ranges (2026):**
- 1 BHK local shift (within 10 km): ₹3,000–₹6,000
- 2 BHK local shift: ₹5,000–₹10,000
- Cross-city shift (e.g., Delhi to Noida): ₹7,000–₹15,000+

Always get a written quote before confirming — and ask what's included (loading, unloading, packing materials).

## Step 5: On Moving Day

A few things to do on the day of your move:

- **Be present** during loading — supervise the process
- **Take photos** of valuable items before they're loaded (for insurance purposes)
- **Check every room** before the vehicle leaves — don't leave anything behind
- **Confirm the driver has the correct destination address**
- **Keep valuables (documents, jewellery, cash) with you** — don't put them in the transport vehicle

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Booking the Cheapest Option Without Checking
Unverified transport providers may damage goods, overcharge on arrival, or simply not show up. Always check:
- GST registration
- Years in business
- Customer reviews

### Not Accounting for Lift/Staircase Time
Loading from a 5th floor with no lift takes significantly longer than ground floor. Factor this into your schedule and confirm with your transport provider.

### Packing Too Late
Packing a 2 BHK takes 6–8 hours if done properly. Start 2–3 days before your move date, not the night before.

### Forgetting to Inform Key Parties
Before you move, notify:
- Your bank (address update)
- Aadhaar/PAN (address update)
- Employer
- Children's school
- Utility providers (electricity, gas, internet)
