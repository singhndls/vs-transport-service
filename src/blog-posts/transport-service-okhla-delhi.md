# Transport Service in Okhla: Fast Logistics for Delhi's Industrial Hub

Okhla Industrial Area is one of Delhi's most important commercial zones. Spread across three phases, it houses thousands of manufacturing units, export houses, garment factories, and warehouses. Every day, hundreds of tonnes of goods move in and out of Okhla — and getting that logistics right is critical for business.

VS Transport Service is headquartered in Okhla Phase 2, which means we know these roads, these lanes, and these timelines better than anyone.

## Why Okhla Needs Specialised Transport

Okhla isn't like a residential colony. The logistics challenges here are unique:

- **Narrow internal roads** in Phase 1 and Phase 2 require smaller vehicles like Chota Hathi or Bolero Pickup
- **High-frequency movement** — many businesses need daily or twice-daily pickups
- **Time-sensitive deliveries** — export shipments, e-commerce dispatches, and factory-to-factory transfers can't afford delays
- **Mixed cargo** — garments, electronics, auto parts, FMCG goods, and raw materials all need different handling

## Our Services in Okhla

### Chota Hathi Service
Perfect for intra-Okhla movement and short-distance deliveries within Delhi. Ideal for garment bundles, packed boxes, and light industrial goods. Fits through narrow lanes where larger trucks can't go.

### Bolero Pickup Service
The workhorse of Okhla logistics. Handles medium loads — 500 kg to 1.5 tonnes — with ease. Great for factory-to-warehouse runs and daily dispatch operations.

### Fleet Transport
For larger shipments moving out of Okhla to Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, or other NCR cities. We deploy the right vehicle based on your load — from Tata Ace to full-size trucks.
