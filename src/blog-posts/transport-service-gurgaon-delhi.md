# Transport Service from Delhi to Gurgaon: Complete Guide

The Delhi–Gurgaon corridor is one of the busiest logistics routes in North India. Every day, thousands of vehicles carry goods between factories in Okhla and Manesar, warehouses in Dwarka and Cyber City, and retail outlets across both cities.

If you are moving goods — whether for a business or a household shift — this guide covers everything you need to know about transport between Delhi and Gurgaon.

## Why Delhi–Gurgaon Is a Unique Route

Gurgaon (officially Gurugram) sits about 30 km south-west of central Delhi. The route looks straightforward on a map, but in practice it involves:

- **NH-48 (Delhi–Jaipur Highway):** The main artery, but heavily congested during peak hours (8–10 AM and 6–9 PM)
- **Dwarka Expressway:** A faster alternative for goods coming from West Delhi or Dwarka
- **Toll plazas:** Two toll points on NH-48 add to cost and time
- **Gurgaon's internal roads:** Cyber City, MG Road, and Sohna Road areas have narrow service lanes that require smaller vehicles

Understanding these factors helps you choose the right vehicle and schedule your pickup time wisely.

## Best Vehicle for Delhi–Gurgaon Transport

### Chota Hathi (Mini Truck)
**Best for:** 1–2 BHK household shifting, small retail deliveries, e-commerce stock  
**Capacity:** Up to 750 kg, approximately 200 cubic feet  
**Advantage:** Fits into narrow Gurgaon lanes and basement parking areas  
**Typical cost:** ₹800–₹1,400 depending on exact pickup and drop points

### Bolero Pickup
**Best for:** 2–3 BHK shifting, factory dispatches, furniture delivery  
**Capacity:** Up to 1,200 kg  
**Advantage:** Faster loading, better for bulky items  
**Typical cost:** ₹1,200–₹2,000

### Large Truck (Fleet)
**Best for:** Full office relocation, bulk commercial goods, industrial equipment  
**Capacity:** 2–5 tonnes  
**Advantage:** Single trip for large volumes  
**Typical cost:** Quoted on request based on load and distance

## How to Reduce Transit Time on This Route

**Book early morning:** A 7–8 AM pickup from Okhla or South Delhi reaches Gurgaon before the NH-48 rush. The same trip at 9 AM can take 45 minutes longer.

**Specify your Gurgaon address clearly:** Gurgaon's sector numbering and DLF Phase system confuses many drivers. Always share the Google Maps pin along with the address.

**Avoid Friday evenings:** The Delhi–Gurgaon highway sees its worst congestion on Friday evenings as people head out for the weekend.

**Use Dwarka Expressway for West Delhi origins:** If your goods are coming from Dwarka, Uttam Nagar, or Janakpuri, the Dwarka Expressway bypasses the NH-48 toll and is often 20–30 minutes faster.
