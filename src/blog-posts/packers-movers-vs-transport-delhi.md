# Packers and Movers in Delhi: What to Know Before You Book

Every year, thousands of families in Delhi NCR shift homes — and a large number of them end up paying more than expected, or worse, receive damaged goods. The packers and movers industry in Delhi is unregulated, which means anyone with a tempo and a phone number can call themselves a moving company.

This guide will help you ask the right questions, spot red flags, and make a smart decision before you hand over your belongings.

## Why Most Shifting Problems Happen

The root cause of most bad shifting experiences is not the move itself — it is the booking process. People compare prices on aggregator apps, pick the cheapest option, and assume the job will be done professionally.

What they do not realise:

- The quoted price is often a base rate that excludes packing material, stairs, and fuel
- Many small operators have no insurance and no accountability
- "Verified" badges on apps do not mean the company is reliable

## 5 Things to Check Before Booking

### 1. Ask for a Written Quote

Any serious transport company will give you a written breakdown — vehicle type, packing material cost, labour charges, and any extra fees for stairs or long-carry distance. If a company only gives you a verbal quote over the phone, that is a warning sign.

At VS Transport Service, every booking comes with a clear written estimate before we start. No surprises on delivery day.

### 2. Confirm the Vehicle Type for Your Load

A 1 BHK typically fits in a Chota Hathi (mini truck). A 2–3 BHK usually needs a Bolero Pickup or a larger vehicle. If a company quotes you a very low price, check whether they are planning to send a vehicle that is too small — which means multiple trips and double the cost.

### 3. Check If Packing Is Included

Some companies quote a transport price and then charge separately for boxes, bubble wrap, and tape. Ask specifically: "Is packing material included in this price?" If not, factor that into your comparison.

### 4. Ask About Insurance

Professional movers should be able to advise you on transit insurance for high-value items — electronics, antiques, glassware. If a company says "nothing will break, don't worry," that is not a policy. That is a promise they cannot keep.

### 5. Verify the Company Has a Physical Address

A company with a real office, a GST number, and a verifiable address is far more accountable than one that operates only through a mobile number. VS Transport Service is based at Z-213, Okhla Phase 2, Harkesh Nagar, New Delhi — you can visit us before booking.

## Red Flags to Watch Out For

**Extremely low quotes:** If a company is 40–50% cheaper than everyone else, they are either cutting corners on packing, planning to overcharge on delivery, or both.

**No written confirmation:** Legitimate companies confirm bookings in writing — via WhatsApp, email, or a printed receipt.

**Pressure to pay full amount upfront:** A standard advance is 20–30%. Paying 100% before the move gives you no leverage if something goes wrong.

**No response to damage claims:** Search the company name + "complaint" or "review" online before booking. Patterns of unresolved complaints are a strong signal.
