# How to Choose a Reliable Transport Company in Delhi NCR

Delhi NCR has no shortage of transport providers. Open any classifieds site and you'll find hundreds of listings — from individual truck owners to large fleet operators. But not all of them are equal, and making the wrong choice can mean delayed deliveries, damaged goods, or unexpected charges.

Having served businesses and households across Delhi NCR for years, we know exactly what separates a trustworthy transport partner from a risky one. Here are 8 checks you should make before handing over your goods.

## 1. Verify They Have a Physical Office

This is the most basic — and most overlooked — check. A transport company with a real office address is accountable. If something goes wrong, you know where to find them.

Ask for their office address and verify it on Google Maps. A company that only operates via a mobile number and has no fixed address is a red flag.

## 2. Check Their Fleet

A company that owns its vehicles is more reliable than one that depends entirely on hired trucks. Owned fleets mean:

- Consistent vehicle quality
- Faster availability
- Better maintenance standards
- Direct accountability for the driver

Ask: "Do you own your vehicles or hire them?" The answer tells you a lot.

## 3. Read Google Reviews — Carefully

Don't just look at the star rating. Read the actual reviews. Look for:

- Mentions of on-time delivery
- How they handled problems or complaints
- Whether the pricing matched the quote
- Comments about the driver's behaviour

A company with 50 genuine 4-star reviews is more trustworthy than one with 200 reviews that all sound identical.

## 4. Ask for a Written Quote

Any serious transport company will give you a written quote — via WhatsApp, email, or a formal document. The quote should clearly state:

- Vehicle type
- Load capacity
- Distance covered
- Total price
- Any additional charges (toll, loading labour, waiting time)

If a company only gives verbal quotes and refuses to put anything in writing, walk away.

## 5. Confirm They Have Experience with Your Type of Goods

Not all transport companies handle all types of goods equally well. A company that specialises in garment transport may not be the best choice for heavy machinery — and vice versa.

Ask specifically: "Have you transported [type of goods] before?" and "What precautions do you take for fragile/heavy/perishable items?"

## 6. Check Response Time

How quickly does the company respond to your inquiry? A company that takes 24 hours to reply to a WhatsApp message will likely be slow when you have an urgent shipment.

Test this before you commit. Call or message them and see how long it takes to get a response and a quote.

## 7. Ask About Insurance and Liability

What happens if your goods are damaged in transit? A professional transport company will have a clear answer. Ask:

- Do you carry goods-in-transit insurance?
- What is your liability policy for damaged goods?
- How do you handle claims?

You don't need a full insurance policy for every small shipment, but for high-value goods, this question is essential.

## 8. Look for Transparent Pricing

Hidden charges are the most common complaint in the transport industry. Common surprise charges include:

- Extra toll charges not mentioned upfront
- "Waiting charges" if loading takes longer than expected
- Additional labour charges for upper floors
- Fuel surcharges on long-distance routes

A trustworthy company tells you about these upfront. Always ask: "Is this the final price, or are there any additional charges I should know about?"

## Red Flags to Watch Out For

- No physical office address
- Refuses to give a written quote
- Price is significantly lower than all competitors (often a sign of hidden charges or poor service)
- No reviews or only very recent reviews
- Driver is different from the one confirmed at booking
- Asks for full payment before the job is done
